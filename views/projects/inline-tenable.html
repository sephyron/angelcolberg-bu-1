Cyber Exposure Management: Lumin and Nessus

I worked on Tenable's SecOps analytics platform across Lumin and Nessus. Security teams had plenty of vulnerability data but needed a faster way to decide what mattered, whether exposure was improving, and what to do next. Use Case

Lumin: Cyber Exposure Scoring

Problem Definition

Security teams tracked vulnerability data across many disconnected reports, making it hard to answer a basic question: is our exposure getting better or worse, and compared to whom.

Hypothesis

A single Cyber Exposure Score, benchmarked against enterprise and population peers over time, would give teams a clear signal of risk trend and a starting point for prioritization.

Method

Service maps and job stories identified the core investigation flow: check the score, compare the trend, see the top actions to reduce it, then drill into assessment and remediation maturity. Mockups and code prototypes were used to validate the layout with stakeholders and users.

Result and next steps

The dashboard paired the CES gauge and trend line with a ranked "Actions to Reduce CES" panel, letter-grade maturity indicators for assessment and remediation, and a live Cyber Exposure Alerts feed, so teams could move from a single score to a specific next step without leaving the page.
