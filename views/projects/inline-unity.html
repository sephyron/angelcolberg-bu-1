Unity

Building a design foundation

At Unity, I set up the design culture and the practical processes that let research and product design scale. I built and coached teams toward a more evidence-driven approach, then worked across groups on shared taxonomies and design-system patterns. The goal was not to standardize for its own sake; it was to give teams enough common ground to make better product decisions faster.

Creating a design system

I started a pattern library for the Ads and Analytics teams and helped grow it into a company-wide capability. I used the real product work to decide which patterns deserved to be shared, documented the interaction rules, and created a feedback loop so the system could evolve with the teams using it.
