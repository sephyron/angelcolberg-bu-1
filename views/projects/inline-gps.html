Fantasy Interactive

Early Material Design implementations for Google Partners

In 2014, Fantasy Interactive brought me in to design a Google Partners search portal before Material Design had been publicly introduced. I translated early guidance into a complete search-to-result experience: a plain-language entry point, guided qualification, comparable partner results, and detailed profiles. I also built front-end prototypes so we could test whether the visual system held up in the actual flow rather than only in static layouts.

Use Case

Partner search entry

Make the first question clear

The opening concept frames the partner-finding task around a plain-language question and a small set of paths. The visual hierarchy gives the user a clear starting point before introducing filters and results.

Result

The experience made the intent of the search flow understandable before users committed to a path.

Guided qualification

Turn qualification into a conversation

The animated concept shows the progressive question flow for visitors who do not yet know which service or partner profile they need.

Result

Progressive disclosure kept the decision surface focused while still supporting multiple business goals.

Help category selection

Separate distinct needs

The category screen lets visitors choose advertising, website, or combined support using large, scannable options with a clear active state.

Result

The choices reduce ambiguity and give the later search results a useful intent signal.

Partner results

Make comparison possible

The results layout puts the number of matches, sort controls, refinement, and search in one row above comparable partner records.

Result

Users can scan, refine, and compare without losing the context of their original request.

Partner profile

Expose evidence for a decision

The profile combines rating, budget, certifications, contact details, and location so a visitor can evaluate a partner without returning to the list.

Result

Decision-making information is grouped into a single readable profile surface.

Agency discovery

Support broad discovery

This screen shifts from a single partner view to the broader agency-finding experience, keeping the search prompt visible while introducing the directory.

Result

The directory supports both guided entry and direct exploration.

Search and refinement

Refine without losing the list

Sort, refine, and search controls remain visible above the partner records, making filtering a reversible part of browsing rather than a separate destination.

Result

Visitors can narrow the set while maintaining orientation in the result list.

Partner detail metrics

Balance narrative and proof

The detail view pairs the partner story with concrete measures such as budget, rating, certifications, and location.

Result

The screen supports both quick confidence and deeper evaluation.

Certification detail

Make qualifications legible

Certification rows expose the capabilities behind a partner’s score and let visitors inspect the evidence supporting the profile.

Result

Qualification details become part of the decision flow instead of hidden metadata.

Responsive location data

Connect partner choice to place

The location-oriented view grounds the search in geography, helping users evaluate agencies in relation to their market and service area.

Result

Location becomes a useful filter and decision aid rather than a decorative detail.
