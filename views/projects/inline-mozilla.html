The Coral Project

I served as Principal UX Lead on The Coral Project, a Knight Foundation-funded initiative created with Mozilla, The New York Times, and The Washington Post. I worked on the open-source design direction and the practical tools publishers could use to build healthier communities around their journalism. The work brought together newsroom research, interaction design, visual systems, and front-end prototypes rather than treating community health as an abstract content problem.

Designing an open product foundation

The available project assets are identity and process artifacts rather than interface screens. The Coral work connected publisher needs, community health, research, and reusable design patterns into an open-source product foundation.

Result

The design direction supported healthier communities while giving publishers a framework they could adapt to their own editorial contexts.
