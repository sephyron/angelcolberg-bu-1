One Microsoft

Revitalizing the Microsoft Visual Identity

I designed the visual and interaction language for One Microsoft, the effort to make Microsoft's internal properties feel like one connected experience. I developed identity principles, reusable patterns, content guidance, and templates that teams could adapt without losing the shared system. The work built on the information model, profile store, and messaging framework established through MicroTalk and NGI, and extended the identity into signage and physical workspaces.
